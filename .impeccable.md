# Design Context — 赛博拼豆 (Bead Studio)

> Derived from the product brief (the user's own words + the reference site fori-flower-studio.com), not from the codebase.
> If any line below is wrong, tell me and the UI will be re-tuned; the 3D simulation is unaffected.

## Audience
Chinese-speaking young adults (≈18–35) who like low-stakes, calming crafts (拼豆, 手账, 插花, 解压小游戏) and who
arrive from a shared video/clip on social feeds. Skill range: never-done-it → veterans who know 色号 by heart.
Laptop first (mouse/trackpad), real mobile support (touch).

## Use
One sitting, 10–40 minutes: pick a board → get beads → place them one by one → iron → finish → keep / share.
They want to *feel* the real craft (the click of a bead on a peg, the hiss of the iron, the sinking feeling of an
over-melt), end with a one-of-a-kind object, and show it to somebody.

## Personality
Calm, tactile, patient, warm, quietly playful. "TAKE YOUR TIME." Mistakes are charming, never punishing.
The product is a real craft table by a window — the UI is a quiet paper companion beside it.
Mantra (from the reference): there is no standard answer; the way you like it is the good way.

## Aesthetic direction — "晨光里的手作桌" (the craft table in morning light)
- Refined/editorial + tactile. Warm paper surfaces, ink-dark warm text, one vermilion accent (朱砂, like a seal stamp),
  and the *beads themselves* supply all the colour variety. No neon, no cyber-glow — "赛博" = digital twin of a real craft.
- The 3D table is the hero (oak, soft window light, real plastic). UI stays out of its way: one left paper panel,
  floating minimal controls, generous negative space.
- Distinctive details: stage stepper drawn as a string of beads; colour chips that look like bead-jar lids;
  paper-slip toasts; a vermilion "NO. 0xx" seal on the finished piece; hand-lettered micro-notes.
- Typography: Fraunces (soft editorial serif, Latin + numerals) with Noto Serif SC for display Chinese;
  Instrument Sans + Noto Sans SC for UI text; tabular figures for bead counts and 色号.
  Font stacks must degrade gracefully to PingFang SC / Microsoft YaHei (Google Fonts can be blocked in mainland China).
- Colour (OKLCH): paper oklch(96% .012 85), surface oklch(98.5% .008 85), ink oklch(24% .02 60), muted ink oklch(46% .02 60),
  hairline oklch(88% .015 80), accent vermilion oklch(60% .185 33). Neutrals are always warm-tinted; never pure black/white.

## Principles
1. Physical truth first: every tool, material, sound and flaw should come from the real offline process
   (docs/research/offline-flow.md). Delight comes from realism, not decoration.
2. Every person's result is different: free choice + physical randomness (bead tolerances, hand shake, heat, pressure).
3. Flaws are part of the fun: show them, name them (赛博义诊), let people live with or fix them.
4. Quiet UI, loud materials. Copy is short, warm, and speaks like a friendly shop assistant.
5. Works with sound off, with touch, with reduced motion; never blocks the craft behind a modal.
